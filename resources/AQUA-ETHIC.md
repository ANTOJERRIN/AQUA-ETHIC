What are you Building?<br>AQUA-ETHIC  is a smart, low-cost water quality monitoring system that combines  Satellite Imaging, IoT, Blockchain, and AI  to<br>detect river pollution in real time. Satellite data first identifies water bodies showing unusual changes. Low-cost solar-powered IoT<br>buoys then continuously monitor  pH, turbidity, temperature, and dissolved oxygen . Every sensor reading is securely stored on the<br>Ethereum blockchain<br>, making it transparent, tamper-proof, and independently verifiable. A live dashboard displays water quality<br>updates, while instant alerts notify citizens and authorities whenever pollution crosses safe limits. Our goal is to make water quality<br>monitoring  faster, affordable, transparent, and scalable  for communities across India.<br>What are the 3-4 Key Features?
